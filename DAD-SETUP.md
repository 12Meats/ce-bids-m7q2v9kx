# CE Bids: Getting Started

The app link:

**https://12meats.github.io/ce-bids-m7q2v9kx/**

## Put it on your home screen (do this FIRST)

Do this first. What you enter in Safari does not carry over to the home screen app.

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** and go to the link above. Adrian can text it to you, just tap it.
2. Tap the **Share button**, the square with the arrow pointing up at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen."**
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right.
5. A navy **CE** icon is now on your home screen. Open it from there from now on, not from Safari.

**Do not delete that icon. Deleting it deletes every bid, every price and every photo on this phone.** The backup you send Adrian is the only copy there is. Photos do not ride along with it; those come out through **Settings › Backup › Export photos**, one job at a time.

## First time you open it

It asks you to **choose a PIN**. Enter it twice. **Write it down, and tell Adrian what it is. There is no way to look it up.**

Then tap **Settings** at the bottom and go through these, in this order:

1. **Bid numbers.** Tap **Next bid number** and set this to your real next invoice number.
2. **Crew.** Shawn at $32 and George at $30 are already in there. Check both, then **+ Add** anyone else with their hourly wage.
3. **Rates & percentages.** **Labor rate an hour, new bids** and the **Rate floor** both come set to **$85**, what UDA pays. The floor is the lowest rate worth working for. **Hours in a work day** should be 8. Leave every percentage alone for now.
4. **Equipment.** Tap each tool and put in a **Cost new**. A tool with no cost asks for one the first time you use it.
5. **Company.** Check the phone, the address, and the ROC number. Pick a **Proposal style**: **Blue & logo** or **Plain black**.

**Terms library** and **Did you forget** are already filled in. Nothing to do there.

## Your first bid

1. On the **Bids** tab, tap **+ New bid**. Put in the customer, a title, the date, and tap **Service** or **Project**. Tap **Start the walk →**.
2. **The walk.** Add an area, like a room or a panel. Tap **+ Item**, then tap a tile or use the **search box at the top**. Type it however you say it: **1"** and **1 in** find the same part. Tap **Photos** to shoot pictures into that area. Tap **Notes** to write down what you saw in that room, or hold the **mic** key and say it. Those notes are yours: they never go on the customer's paper. Before you leave, answer **Anything missing?**. Tap **Next: Labor →**.

   Ask the plant before you price it: are we live or shut down, and what runs while it's down? Generators and after-hours work go on the bid.

   A **rental** is entered as the **total**, not a price per day. A lift at $285 for the week is $285; the days are just what goes on the paper.
3. **Labor.** Pick who is on the job and how many days. Different pieces? Tap **Split into tasks** and give each its own crew and days. Tap **Costs & price**.
4. **Costs & price.** Three handles are all you touch: **Material markup %**, **Hours cushion %**, and **Margin %**. Watch the line showing what rate this labor works out to. Red means you dropped under your floor, so back a handle off. Tap **Proposal**.

   On a **Schreiber** bid they are still at $65, so tap **Labor rate** and set 65 there. The red below-your-floor line is expected until their rate comes up, and it changes that bid only.
5. **Proposal.** Pick the detail: **Full**, **Summary**, or **Scope & price**. Add notes or exclusions. On a project bid tap **Choose clauses**. Write the **Scope of work**.
6. **Share proposal**, then **Mail**. It asks **"Sent to the customer?"** and **"Did you save a copy on the phone?"**. Answer both. Your home screen now shows the bid as **Sent**.

When you save to Files, put it in **iCloud Drive › Cantu Electric › Bids › 2026**.

## Getting around

**Back goes one step.** The **‹** at the top left backs out one screen at a time: a part list to the room, the room to the walk, the walk to the bid. The swipe from the left edge does the same. Nothing is lost, and neither one closes the app.

## When you hear back

Open the bid and tap **Won** or **Lost**. They sit side by side.

Won a bid? Tap **Job**. That is where you:

- Put in the hours each week as the guys work.
- Log a **Surprise** when something unexpected costs you money.
- Add a **Change order** when they ask for extra work. One can be labor only: with no parts on it, just tap **Next: Labor**.
- Tap **Mark complete** when the job is done.

Wrong button? Until you log hours there is a one tap **Undo Won** or **Reopen** on the bid.

## Every couple of weeks

Go to **Settings › Backup › Send a backup to Adrian**. His email is copied for you, so just paste it in. The home screen nags you after two weeks. If the phone is lost or replaced, that file is how we get it all back.

## Reports

Once three jobs are finished, **Settings › Reports › Open reports** starts telling you things: your win rate, bid hours against real hours, and what your margin actually came out to.

**What an hour costs** is one page you can print. It shows what an hour of your crew has to bill to make money. That is the page for the rate conversation.

## If something looks wrong

- At the bottom of **Settings** is a line reading **"CE Bids · v2 · built …"**. Read it to Adrian. If it still says v1, close the app all the way and open it again.
- If a red bar says storage was unreadable, go to **Settings › Backup › Restore from backup**.
- A yellow **"Nobody on this task"** means a task has days but nobody on it. It still charges truck and gas, so add the guys or take the days off.
