# CE Bids: Getting Started

The app link:

**https://12meats.github.io/ce-bids-m7q2v9kx/**

## Put it on your home screen (do this FIRST)

What you enter in Safari does not carry over to the home screen app.

1. Open **Safari** and go to the link above. Adrian can text it to you.
2. Tap the **Share button** at the bottom, the square with the arrow pointing up.
3. Scroll down, tap **"Add to Home Screen,"** then **"Add"**.
4. Open the navy **CE** icon from your home screen from now on, not from Safari.

**Do not delete that icon. Deleting it deletes every bid, every price and every photo on this phone.** The backup you send Adrian is the only other copy. Photos go separately, through **Settings › Backup › Export photos**, one job at a time.

## First time you open it

It asks you to **choose a PIN**. Enter it twice. **Write it down, and tell Adrian what it is. There is no way to look it up.**

Then tap **Settings**. It is a list of doors: a row with a **›** opens its own screen, and **‹ Settings** brings you back. Go through these, in order:

1. **Bid numbers.** The counter starts at **#1**. Set **Next bid number** to your real next invoice number before the first bid.
2. **Crew ›.** Shawn at $32 and George at $30 are in already. Check both, then **+ Worker** for anyone else and his wage.
3. **Rates ›.** **Labor rate** and **Rate floor** both come set to **$85**, what UDA pays. The floor is the lowest rate worth working for. **Hours per day** should be 8. Leave the other percentages alone.

   **Material markup** should read **15%**. If yours says 18, tap it and put in 15. Bids you already wrote keep the markup they were figured at.
4. **Equipment ›.** Thirty tools. Tap one and put in a **Cost new**. A tool with no cost asks for one the first time you use it.
5. **Company ›.** Check the phone, the address, and the ROC number. Pick a **Proposal style**: **Blue & logo** or **Plain black**. **Supply house search** is where the **Check price** link points.

**Parts catalog** (210 parts), **Terms library** (27 clauses), **Anything missing** and **Note phrases** come filled in. Nothing to do there.

**Every number in Rates is new bids only.** Change the labor rate today and it is what the next bid starts at. A bid you already wrote keeps the numbers it was figured at, so nothing you handed a customer moves behind your back.

## Your first bid

1. On the **Bids** tab, tap **+ New bid**. Put in the customer, a title, the date, and tap **Service** or **Project**. Tap **Start the walk →**.
2. **The walk.** Add an area, like a room or a panel. Tap **+ Item**, then a tile or the **search box**. Type it however you say it: **1"** and **1 in** find the same part. **Photos** shoots pictures into that area. **Notes** is where you write what you saw, or hold the **mic** key and say it. Those notes are yours: they never go on the customer's paper.

   When it asks a part's cost, **Check price** opens your supply house in Safari with the name typed in. It needs signal, and the keypad waits for you.

   **Two prices on a part.** Tap a line, then **Bills at** to put the list price on it: the price the customer is billed, before the markup. Cost stays what you paid. Tap **Price the whole line instead** on that keypad to give the line one number for all of it, the way your invoices do a roll of wire ($216.00 for 500 ft prints as 500 ft with no price per foot, just $216.00). **Clear** on the whole-line keypad puts the line back to a price per foot. **Clear** on the **Bills at** keypad puts it back to billing off its cost. Once you put a bill price on a part, the next line you add for it starts there.

   Before you leave, answer **Anything missing?**. It shows the six you forget most, and **Show all 19** opens the rest. **Not this job** puts a row away. **Add it** puts it on the bid: a lift or a dumpster goes through the rental question, anything else asks the price there and then.

   Ask the plant before you price it: are we live or shut down, and what runs while it's down? Generators and after-hours work go on the bid.

   A **rental** is the **total**, not a price per day. A lift at $285 for the week is $285; the days only go on the paper.
3. **Labor.** Pick who is on the job and how many days. Different pieces? **Split into tasks** gives each its own crew and days.
4. **Costs & price.** The top of the screen is what the job costs you. The bottom is the price, and it has three handles: **Margin %**, **Labor rate**, and **Bid price**. Move one and the other two follow. Watch the line showing what rate this labor works out to. Red means you dropped under your floor, so back a handle off.

   Everything you type here changes **this bid only**. If a draft has gone stale, **Use today's Settings on this bid** pulls it back onto the shop numbers and says what moves first.

   On a **Schreiber** bid set the **Labor rate** to 65. They are still there, and the red floor line is expected until it comes up.
5. **Proposal.** Pick the detail: **Full**, **Summary**, or **Scope & price**. Add notes or exclusions. A scope and price bid starts with your standard terms on it; on the others tap **Choose clauses** if the job needs them. Write the **Scope of work**.
6. **Share proposal**, then **Mail**. It asks **"Sent to the customer?"** and **"Did you save a copy on the phone?"**. Answer both, and the bid reads **Sent**. Saving to Files? Put it in **iCloud Drive › Cantu Electric › Bids › 2026**.

## Getting around

**Back goes one step.** The **‹** at the top left backs out one screen at a time: a part list to the room, the room to the walk, the walk to the bid. The left-edge swipe does the same, and with a keypad or a menu open it closes only that.

## When you hear back

Open the bid and tap **Won** or **Lost**. They sit side by side.

Won a bid? Tap **Job**. That is where you:

- Put in the hours each week as the guys work.
- Log a **Surprise** when something unexpected costs you money.
- Add a **Change order** for extra work. One can be labor only: with no parts on it, just tap **Next: Labor**.
- Tap **Mark complete** when the job is done.

Wrong button? Until you log hours there is a one tap **Undo Won** or **Reopen** on the bid.

## Every couple of weeks

Go to **Settings › Backup › Send a backup to Adrian**. His email is copied for you, so paste it in. The home screen nags after two weeks. If the phone is lost, that file is how we get it all back.

## Reports

Once three jobs are finished, **Settings › Reports** starts telling you your win rate, bid hours against real hours, and what your margin came out to.

**What an hour costs** is one page you can print. It shows what an hour of your crew has to bill to make money. That is the page for the rate conversation.

## If something looks wrong

- At the bottom of **Settings** is a line reading **"CE Bids · v2.1 · built …"**. Read it to Adrian. If it says an older number, close the app all the way and open it again.
- If a red bar says storage was unreadable, go to **Settings › Backup › Restore from backup**.
- A yellow **"Nobody on this task"** means a task has days but nobody on it. It still charges truck and gas, so add the guys or take the days off.
