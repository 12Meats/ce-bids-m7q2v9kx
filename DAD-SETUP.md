# CE Bids: Getting Started

The app link:

**https://12meats.github.io/ce-bids-m7q2v9kx/**

## Put it on your home screen (do this FIRST)

Do this before you type anything into it. Whatever you enter in Safari does not carry over to the home screen app. They keep separate files.

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** and go to the link above. Adrian can text it to you, just tap it.
2. Tap the **Share button**, the square with the arrow pointing up at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen."**
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right.
5. A navy **CE** icon is now on your home screen. Open it from there from now on, not from Safari.

## First time you open it

It asks you to **choose a PIN**. Enter it twice. Tell Adrian what it is, just in case.

Then tap **Settings** at the bottom and go through these, in this order:

1. **Bid numbers.** Tap **Next bid number** and set this to your real next invoice number.
2. **Crew.** Shawn at $32 and George at $30 are already in there. Check both numbers, then **+ Add** anyone else with their hourly wage.
3. **Rates & percentages.** Set **Labor rate an hour, new bids** to $65. Check the **Rate floor**, the lowest rate worth working for. **Hours in a work day** should be 8. Leave every percentage alone for now. After your first real bid, we compare it to your hand number and adjust from there.
4. **Equipment.** Tap each tool and put in a **Cost new**. Any tool without a cost will ask you for it the first time you use it on a bid.
5. **Company.** Check the phone, the address, and the ROC number. Pick a **Proposal style**: **Blue & logo** or **Plain black**.

**Terms library** and **Did you forget** are already filled in. Nothing to do there.

## Your first bid

1. On the **Bids** tab, tap **+ New bid**. Put in the customer, a title, the date, and tap **Service** or **Project**. Tap **Start the walk →**.
2. **The walk.** Add an area, like a room or a panel. Tap the tiles for what goes in it and enter the counts. Tap **Photos** to shoot pictures right into that area. Before you leave, open **Did you forget?** and answer the questions. Tap **Next: Labor →**.
3. **Labor.** Pick who is on the job and how many days. If the job has different pieces, tap **Split into tasks** and give each one its own crew and days. Tap **Costs & price**.
4. **Costs & price.** Three handles are all you touch: **Material markup %**, **Hours cushion %**, and **Margin %**. Watch the line that shows what rate this labor works out to. If it turns red, you dropped under your floor, so back a handle off. Tap **Proposal**.
5. **Proposal.** Pick how much detail: **Full**, **Summary**, or **Scope & price**. Add notes or exclusions. On a project bid, tap **Choose clauses** for the terms. Write the **Scope of work**.
6. **Share proposal**, then **Mail**. It asks **"Sent to the customer?"** and **"Saved to Files?"**. Answer both. Your home screen now shows the bid as **Sent**.

When you save to Files, put it in **iCloud Drive › Cantu Electric › Bids › 2026**.

## When you hear back

Open the bid and tap **Won** or **Lost**. They sit side by side.

Won a bid? Tap **Job**. That is where you:

- Put in the hours each week as the guys work.
- Log a **Surprise** when something unexpected costs you money.
- Add a **Change order** when they ask for extra work.
- Tap **Mark complete** when the job is done.

Hit the wrong button? Until you have logged hours, there is a one tap **Undo Won** or **Reopen** right there on the bid.

## Every couple of weeks

Go to **Settings › Backup › Send backup**. Adrian's email is copied for you, so just paste it in. The home screen will nag you once it has been two weeks.

Everything lives on this phone. If the phone is lost or replaced, that backup file is how we get it all back.

## Reports

Once you have finished three jobs, **Settings › Reports › Open reports** starts telling you things: your win rate, how your bid hours compared to real hours, and what your margin actually came out to.

**What an hour costs** is one page you can print. It shows what an hour of your crew has to bill to make money. That is the page for the rate conversation.

## If something looks wrong

- At the bottom of **Settings** there is a line that reads **"CE Bids · version …"**. Read that line to Adrian. It tells him exactly what you are running.
- If a red bar says storage was unreadable, go to **Settings › Backup › Restore from backup**.
- A yellow **"Nobody on this task"** means a task has days on it but nobody assigned. It still charges truck and gas for those days, so either add the guys or take the days off.
