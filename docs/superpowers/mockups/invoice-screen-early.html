How the two kinds differ on this screen

  • T&M (shown): labor lines come from the log with billed hours editable beside the logged hours; materials at cost with the markup switch per line; "Work done" is the log's one line.
  • Project: the Labor and Materials cards are replaced by one line, "Cheese plant lighting, as proposed #1057 · $13,030.00", plus a line per approved change order; "Bill part of it" lets him type a smaller amount for a big job and the rest stays open on the bid.
  • Both: Send invoice shares the PDF the way proposals do, marks it Sent with the date, and it lands in the invoice list waiting for a payment.
